\ChapterPage{Page 8 \textbullet{} Field Notes}{Instructions for the Next Watch}
\begin{fieldnote}{If the Lighthouse Turns Twice}
Do not approach the lamp room until dawn. Mark the time by a pocket watch
kept outside the tower. If both watches disagree, trust neither. Record the
weather, the direction of the wind, and whether the gulls are facing land.
\end{fieldnote}

\begin{fieldnote}{If the Water Climbs the Stairs}
Leave the key in the lock. Do not pull it free. Keep one hand on the rail
and one hand on the chart. If the chart begins to warm, read nothing aloud.
Names spoken below the arch may return with different owners.
\end{fieldnote}

\begin{fieldnote}{A Note Added in Another Ink}
The instructions above were written by Mara Bell. The final sentence is in
Dr. Voss's hand, though he denies writing it:
\textit{The door is not beneath the harbor. The harbor is above the door.}
\end{fieldnote}
