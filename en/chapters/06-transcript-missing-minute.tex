\ChapterPage{Page 6 \textbullet{} Transcript}{The Missing Minute}
\noindent\textbf{VALE:} What happened when the bell sounded?\\
\textbf{BELL:} The passage filled with light. Not bright light. A light
that made every shadow look like a doorway.\\
\textbf{VALE:} How many strokes?\\
\textbf{BELL:} Three.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} Four.\\
\textbf{VALE:} You disagree.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} We disagree about the order. The fourth came first.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{VALE:} The town clock stopped at 3:33.\\
\textbf{BELL:} It stopped because it heard the bell.\\
\textbf{VALE:} Clocks do not hear.\\
\textbf{BELL:} This one did.
\end{quote}

\textbf{VALE:} What became of the chart?\\
\textbf{BELL:} The paper was blank when we reached the street.\\
\textbf{VALE:} And the bottle?\\
\textbf{BELL:} It was still dry.\\
\textbf{VALE:} The record shows a pause of fifty-nine seconds here.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} Your clock lost a minute.\\
\textbf{VALE:} My clock is keeping time.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} So is the tide.

\medskip
\noindent\textit{At this point the recording contains a low tone. The clerk
has written no further questions. The transcript ends with the words
``door open'' in a hand unlike his own.}
