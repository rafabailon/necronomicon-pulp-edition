\ChapterPage{Pages 5--6 \textbullet{} Transcript}{Interview at the Customs House}
\textsc{Recorded by Clerk J. Vale, October 4, 1928. The following transcript
is reconstructed from notes; pauses are marked where the gramophone needle
failed.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{VALE:} State your name for the record.\\
\textbf{BELL:} Mara Bell.\\
\textbf{VALE:} And the doctor?\\
\textbf{VOSS:} Elian Voss. I am here as a witness, not a physician.\\
\textbf{VALE:} You entered the cellar?\\
\textbf{BELL:} We went down the stairs. The cellar was not at the bottom.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{VALE:} Explain that distinction.\\
\textbf{BELL:} The stairs ended at a door. The door opened onto a passage
that should not fit beneath the building.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} The architecture is impossible only if one assumes the
building rests on ordinary ground.\\
\textbf{VALE:} What else might it rest on?\\
\textbf{VOSS:} A foundation, perhaps. Or a memory of one.
\end{quote}

\textbf{VALE:} Did either of you see a creature?\\
\textbf{BELL:} We saw a shadow in the glass.\\
\textbf{VALE:} A reflection?\\
\textbf{BELL:} It moved before we did.\\
\textbf{VOSS:} The panes were not reflecting the room. They were reflecting
the tide at a distance we could not measure.
