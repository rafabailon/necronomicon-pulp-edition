\ChapterPage{Pages 7--8 \textbullet{} Field Notes}{Observations in the Salt Book}
\begin{fieldnote}{Field Note 1: Green Glass}
Fragments from the quay are smooth on both sides. Under a lens, the surface
shows tiny parallel grooves. They are not scratches: each groove ends at a
perfectly round pit. Do not place the fragments near a compass. The needle
turns slowly and settles on the cellar stairs.
\end{fieldnote}

\begin{fieldnote}{Field Note 2: The Harbor Bell}
No bell is listed in the harbor inventory. The sound is strongest below the
breakwater and weakest on the lighthouse path. It is not louder at high tide.
It is louder when the tide is absent.
\end{fieldnote}

\begin{fieldnote}{Field Note 3: Chart Measurements}
The second coastline has no scale bar. The ink remains wet only when the
chart is folded toward the sea. Fold it inland and the line fades. A chart
should describe a place. This one appears to instruct the place where to be.
\end{fieldnote}
