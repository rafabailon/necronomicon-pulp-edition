\ChapterPage{Page 10 \textbullet{} Appendix}{A Reader's Caution}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Never assume that a map and the land are describing the same moment.
  \item A closed door is not proof that a passage has ended.
  \item Record observations before interpretations; the latter have a habit of changing.
  \item If the tide arrives from inland, consult no clock in Greyhaven.
\end{enumerate}
