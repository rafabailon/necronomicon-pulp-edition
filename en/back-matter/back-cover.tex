\noindent When Mara Bell discovers a bottle no wave can wet, she finds a
shoreline the world has forgotten. With Dr. Voss at her side, she descends
into the customs house and hears the harbor answer a question no one asked.

\medskip
\noindent A strange, salt-stained adventure of vanished minutes, impossible
maps, and the dark geography beneath familiar streets.
